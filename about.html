About Our Organisation

Founded in 2018 in Gauteng, EcoCare Community Trust has grown into a structured NPO managing community food gardens and conservation workshops across South Africa.

Our Workshop & Team Workspace

Our Mission

To empower local communities through accessible, sustainable environmental education and self-sustaining agriculture.

Our Vision

To foster green, self-reliant urban communities where every neighbourhood possesses active recycling systems.

Our Values

Integrity, transparency, community ownership, and dedicated stewardship of South Africa's natural ecosystems.
